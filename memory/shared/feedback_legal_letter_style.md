For dispute or demand letters (first used on the Side By Side Share ATV claim, 2026-10-20), the user wants:
1. Formal, flowing "lawyer" prose, written in the company's name (e.g. "Dimaskus LLC"), not casual first person.
2. Each opposing point quoted verbatim ("Your position: ..."), then a "Response:" that argues it, then a "Supporting record:" line with at least 3 numbered sources (dated attachments, verbatim contract clauses by heading, the other side's own documents).
3. A summary of asks at the top AND an "In Summary" section at the end listing every request with its dollar amount and the section that supports it.
4. The other side's own documents and terms used against them wherever possible; every quote checked word for word against the source before sending.
5. Fight for every legitimate dollar (he pushed back hard when I suggested dropping smaller claims), but never state a fact the records do not support; word claims so they survive a check of the evidence.

**Why:** he wants letters that could stand up in court and pressure the other side to pay.
**How to apply:** use this structure by default for any claim, dispute or demand letter. Related: [[project_atv_rollover_claim]], [[feedback_no_dashes]].
