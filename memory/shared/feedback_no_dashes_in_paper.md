For the user's papers and written deliverables, use **no em dashes (—) and no en dashes (–)** anywhere. Rewrite sentences to avoid them (commas, colons, periods, parentheses), and write number/date/page ranges as "X to Y" rather than "X–Y".

Also reword ordinary hyphenated compounds where natural (e.g., "non-citizen" to "noncitizen", "forty-six" to "46", "top-down" to "imposed from above"). **Keep** hyphens only where removing them breaks correctness: inside real book titles (e.g., *The Vindolanda Writing-Tablets*) and established proper terms (e.g., "Romano-British").

**Why:** Stated preference, given emphatically ("I dont want any dashes anywhere in my paper"). Chose the aggressive scope when asked, accepting that page ranges written as "89 to 116" are non-standard Chicago.

**How to apply:** Applies to prose AND citations. When a strict citation style would normally require an en dash in a page range, still use "to" but it is worth a one-line flag that this departs from strict Chicago. Sits alongside [[feedback_no_section_sign]] as a hard typographic rule for this user's output.
