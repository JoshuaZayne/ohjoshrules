Ram's **10-year / 100,000-mile powertrain warranty is available to the ORIGINAL (first) owner ONLY.** A second owner (i.e. buying any USED/pre-titled truck) does NOT get it and falls back to the standard transferable **5-year / 60,000-mile** powertrain warranty.

**Why it matters:** this reframes the used-vs-new decision on the [[project_ram3500_autonation_deal]]. Example: the Sam Leman CDJR (Morton IL) used 2026 Limited (VIN 3C63R3PL1TG191942, 3,959 mi, $87,407) looks ~$4k cheaper than AutoNation's NEW gray TG332645 ($91,497), but as a used unit it loses the 10yr/100k on a ~$90k HO Cummins diesel (down to 5yr/60k) - the lost coverage likely eats most/all of the price gap. So buying NEW from AutoNation keeps the full 10yr/100k; the goal is getting a NEW truck near a used truck's price.

**How to apply:** whenever comparing a used Limited to the new AutoNation trucks, flag the warranty gap - don't treat a used truck's lower sticker as a straight discount.
