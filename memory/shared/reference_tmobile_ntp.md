Number Transfer PIN (NTP): 6-digit number, valid for 7 days after being generated. Required to port a T-Mobile number to another carrier.

**Via app or website:**
1. Sign in to My T-Mobile
2. Select Account
3. Select Profile Settings
4. Select Line Settings
5. Select Request a Transfer PIN
6. Follow the verification steps

Note: you must be registered as the Primary Account Holder (PAH) to retrieve an NTP.

**Via phone:**
- Dial 611 or call 800-937-8997
- Request a Number Transfer PIN and follow the verification steps
- Note: you must be able to confirm a One Time PIN sent to your device

**If you can't confirm the One Time PIN:** visit a T-Mobile retail store with a valid ID — the representative will contact the internal team and provide the NTP directly.

**Port center:** 877-778-2106
